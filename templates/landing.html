THE MEALMATCH DIFFERENCE

Meet your MealMatch AI assistant

MealMatch AI is designed to help receivers find food that fits their needs. It compares available listings with each request, ranks suitable options, and explains why a match may work.

  • Compares food type, quantity, price, location, and availability
  • Highlights the strongest matches and explains the fit
  • Helps providers and receivers coordinate pickup details

AI-assisted matching and coordination are planned features; they are not currently active in this version of the app.

FROM SIGNUP TO SHARED MEALS

How MealMatch works

Providers share surplus food. Receivers find options. Together, they coordinate a handover.

  1. 01

    Create an account

    Providers enter their organisation details and FSSAI licence number during signup. This version stores the number but does not yet verify it with the FSSAI registry.

  2. 02

    Share available food

    Providers post the food, quantity, price, location, and pickup availability so receivers can see what is offered.

  3. 03

    Find the best fit

    MealMatch AI is intended to compare listings with receiver needs, rank suitable options, and explain why they match.

  4. 04

    Coordinate and confirm

    The provider and receiver can arrange pickup, communicate about the request, and confirm when the food handover is complete.
